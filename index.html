How Spread NFC Dynamic QR & NFC Works

  1. Activate your physical smart card or acrylic standee at spreadnfc.in/update using your QR serial number and packaging setup passcode.
  2. Choose your permanent experience mode: Personal Multi-Link Profile, Business Direct Link (instant HTTP 302 redirect), or Business Profile.
  3. Sign in anytime with your password to update links, reorder buttons, or manage multiple business QR codes without changing your printed hardware.
